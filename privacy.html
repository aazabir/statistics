Privacy

This book is free and open. You can read every chapter without an account, and nothing you read is tracked to you personally.

Visit counts

This site uses GoatCounter to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal information.

Optional sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, the site receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google. They are used only to show who is signed in.

When you are signed in, the site stores these items so they follow you across your devices:

  • your reading position in each chapter and the last chapter you opened
  • your theme and text size
  • your bookmarks and notes, which stay private to your account

Your data is stored with Google Firebase. Each account can read and write only its own data. I do not sell or share it.

Your choices

Signing in is optional. Signing out stops syncing. To have your stored data deleted, email me and I will remove it.
